Pork Binagoongan

Thin-sliced pork belly, seared, then braised with garlic, onion, tomato and shrimp paste until tender. Pan-fried eggplant is folded in at the end.

4 lb pork belly Boil 45+ min Shrimp paste 2–3 tbsp

Method

  1. Slice and season the pork

    Slice 4 lb pork belly into thin, bite-sized pieces. Season with salt and pepper and mix.

  2. Sear

    Sear the pork in a pan with oil.

  3. Sauté the aromatics

    Sauté 3 cloves of garlic, 1 onion and 3 tomatoes.

  4. Add pork and shrimp paste

    Add the seared pork and mix. Stir in 2 to 3 tbsp shrimp paste. Cook on medium heat for about 15 minutes, mixing so the pork doesn't burn.

  5. Boil until tender

    Add about 3 cups of water and turn up the heat. Boil for at least 45 minutes to cook the pork through and make it tender.

    Keep adding water so it doesn't run dry.
  6. Fry the eggplant

    While the pork boils, cut 8 eggplants into smaller pieces and pan-fry them until cooked. Set aside.

  7. Combine and adjust

    Once the pork is tender, after about 45 minutes, add the fried eggplant and mix it into the sauce. Adjust with salt and pepper if needed.

Cook's notes

  • The pork boils at least 45 minutes; tenderness matters more than the clock.
  • The eggplant is fried separately while the pork boils, then folded in at the end.
