Beef Nilaga

Seared beef shank with its bone marrow, boiled in beef broth until tender, then finished with potatoes, corn or saba, and quick-cooked cabbage, green beans and bok choy.

5 big pieces beef shank Boil 1.5 hr Broth 1 : 1 beef broth : water

Method

  1. Season and sear the shank

    Season the 5 big pieces of beef shank with salt and pepper. Sear them in oil in a frying pan.

  2. Cut the beef

    Once seared, cut the shank into smaller pieces, cutting around the bone marrow so it stays intact.

  3. Sauté with garlic and onion

    In a new pan, sauté garlic and onion. Add the cut beef and the bone marrow over medium heat. Season with fish sauce and ground pepper.

  4. Cook down for 15 minutes

    Sauté on medium heat for 15 minutes, mixing every so often so it doesn't burn.

  5. Add broth and boil 1.5 hours

    Add beef broth and water in about a 1:1 ratio, as much as you want for the soup. Turn the heat up and boil for 1.5 hours until the beef is tender.

    Keep adding water as it boils down.
  6. Add peppercorns and Knorr

    Once the beef is tender, add whole peppercorns and 1 Knorr beef cube.

  7. Add potatoes and corn or saba

    Add peeled, cut potatoes and corn or saba. Boil until the potatoes are almost cooked.

  8. Taste and adjust

    Taste the soup and adjust with fish sauce, pepper and salt.

  9. Add the greens

    Add the cabbage, green beans and bok choy. Cook only briefly. Don't overcook the vegetables at this point.

Cook's notes

  • Seasoned to taste with fish sauce, pepper and salt before adding the greens.
