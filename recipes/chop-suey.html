Chop Suey

Thin-sliced chicken breast in a soy and oyster sauce, thickened with a cornstarch slurry, with eight kinds of vegetables cooked just until crunchy. Quail eggs go in at the end.

1–2 lb chicken breast Water 2 cups 8 vegetables, kept crunchy

Method

  1. Sauté the aromatics

    Sauté 1 onion and 2 bulbs of garlic in avocado oil.

  2. Add and season the chicken

    Add 1 to 2 lb chicken breast cut into thin slices. Season with salt and pepper, then add soy sauce and oyster sauce to taste.

  3. Cook for 10 minutes

    Sauté on medium heat for 10 minutes so the chicken absorbs the flavor.

  4. Add water and boil

    Add 2 cups of water and turn up the heat. Once it boils, add Knorr chicken powder. Mix, then cover until the sauce is slightly reduced.

  5. Add the carrots

    Add 2 to 3 carrots sliced into flat circles and let them cook.

  6. Thicken the sauce

    While the carrots cook, mix cornstarch with water to make a slurry. Stir it into the pan to thicken the sauce.

  7. Add the vegetables

    Add young corn, cauliflower, broccoli, sliced red and green bell pepper, snow peas and sliced cabbage. Cover so the vegetables soften, then mix well.

    Don't overcook the vegetables. They're better crunchy.
  8. Season and finish

    Season to taste with salt and pepper. At the end, add the cooked quail eggs.
