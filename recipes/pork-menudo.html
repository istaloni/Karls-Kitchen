Pork Menudo

Pork shoulder marinated in calamansi and soy, braised in tomato sauce and liver spread, finished with potatoes, carrots and bell pepper. Seasoned to taste at the end, mostly with salt and brown sugar.

4 lb pork Marinate 30 min Marinade 1 : 1 calamansi : soy

Method

  1. Cut and marinate the pork

    Cut 4 lb pork butt or shoulder into small rectangular pieces. In a mixing bowl, combine with calamansi juice and soy sauce in a 1:1 ratio, plus pepper and relish. Marinate 30 minutes.

  2. Prep the aromatics and vegetables

    Crush 2 bulbs of garlic, cut 2 onions and 2 tomatoes. Peel and cube 4 carrots and 3 potatoes, and set them aside for later.

  3. Sauté

    Heat cooking oil. Sauté the onion first, then the garlic, then the tomato.

  4. Add the pork

    Add the pork, leaving as much of the marinade behind as you can. Cook over medium heat in its own juices with the onion, garlic and tomato.

  5. Build the sauce

    Once boiling, add bay leaves, 1 Knorr pork cube and pepper. Then add the tomato sauce, then the liver spread. Mix and cook until the pork is cooked and soft and the sauce has reduced.

  6. Add potatoes and carrots

    Add the cubed carrots and potatoes with 2 cups of water. Boil until they're cooked through and the sauce reduces again.

  7. Add bell pepper

    Add 2 bell peppers cut into small pieces and let them cook.

    Better with 1 green and 1 red than 2 green.
  8. Taste and adjust

    Taste the sauce and adjust with fish sauce, salt, relish and brown sugar. Most of the adjusting was salt and brown sugar.

Cook's notes

  • Needed salt and brown sugar at the end to balance the sauce.
  • Two green bell peppers worked; one green and one red would be better.
