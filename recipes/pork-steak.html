Pork Steak

Skin-on pork loin marinated in lemon and soy, seared, then simmered in its own marinade with onion rings. Sweetened and peppered to taste at the end with brown sugar.

4 lb pork loin Marinate 30+ min Marinade 1 : 1 lemon : soy

Shopping list

Meat & marinade

Onions

Pantry

0 of 7 checked

Method

  1. Marinate the pork

    Squeeze the 7 lemons. Marinate the 4 lb pork loin in equal volumes of lemon juice and soy sauce, with pepper and brown sugar, for at least 30 minutes.

  2. Sear the pork

    Lift the pork out and save the marinade. Sear and fry the pork in a frying pan, then take it out of the pan.

  3. Sauté onion with the pork

    In a new pan, sauté 1 onion cut into small pieces. Add the seared pork and sauté it over medium heat until it picks up the onion flavor.

  4. Simmer in the marinade

    Pour in the rest of the marinade and bring it to a boil so the pork cooks through.

  5. Add onion rings

    While it boils, slice the other 3 big onions into rings and add them to the pan.

  6. Season and finish

    Add brown sugar and pepper a little at a time, tasting as you go, until it tastes right. It's done once the onion rings are cooked.

Cook's notes
